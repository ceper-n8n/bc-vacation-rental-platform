Thoughtful stays, supported by dependable systems.

BC Vacation Rental operates a small collection of vacation homes in Vancouver, British Columbia. This site explains the private tools and Google integrations used behind the scenes to support reservations, guest communication and property operations.

Guest-focused

Automation helps us respond consistently, keep reservation information organized and prepare each property for arriving guests.

Automated tasks support our work; they do not replace human review of guest requests, reservations or important decisions.

What the system supports

Simple connections between the services we already use.

  • ReservationsOrganizing selected booking and guest information from our property-management tools.
  • ContactsKeeping relevant guest contact records current for legitimate hospitality operations.
  • Operational recordsUpdating internal Google Sheets used to coordinate stays and business activity.
  • TasksCreating and maintaining operational reminders for property and guest-service work.
