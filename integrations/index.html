Google integrations

Automation for our own hospitality operations.

BC Vacation Rental uses a privately administered workflow system to connect selected reservation activity with the operator's Google account. Access is authorized by the operator and is used only for our own vacation-rental business.

Contacts

Google Contacts

Relevant guest contact details may be created or updated so the operator can recognize and communicate with guests in connection with their stay.

Records

Google Sheets

Selected reservation and operational information may be added to internal spreadsheets used to coordinate stays and maintain business records.

Tasks

Google Tasks

Operational reminders may be created or updated to support property preparation, follow-up and other legitimate business work.

Data principles

Limited to what the operation needs.

  • Private useThe integration is not offered to outside customers.
  • Purpose limitationGoogle data is used only for the operational purposes described here and in our Privacy Policy.
  • No advertising saleGoogle user data is not sold or used for targeted advertising.
  • Operator controlThe operator authorizes the connection and can revoke it through Google Account security settings.
